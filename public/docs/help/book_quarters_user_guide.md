# Book Quarters: User Guide

## Introduction
The **Book Quarters** page is the gateway for officers to request accommodation. It combines the booking portals with a live inventory of available quarters.

**URL:** `/book-quarters`

---

## 1. Booking Portals
At the top of the page, two distinct green buttons direct you to the specific application forms:

*   **Book Family Quarters:** For long-term accommodation for officers and their families.
*   **Book Scheduled Quarters:** For temporary or specific-purpose accommodation.

## 2. Overview of Quarters
Before applying, you can review the **Quarters Inventory Table** at the bottom of the page to find a suitable vacancy.

### Key Information:
*   **Quarters No:** Both Old and New identification numbers.
*   **Type:** e.g., Family or Bachelor.
*   **Status:** Current condition (e.g., Good, Repair).
*   **Occupancy Rules:**
    *   **Allowed/Max Occupants:** The capacity limit.
    *   **Allowed Gender:** Specific gender restrictions (if any).
*   **Availability:**
    *   **Vacancies:** Calculated as `Max Occupants - Current Occupants`.
    *   If this number is **> 0**, the quarter has space.
*   **Special Notice:** Specific conditions or facilities details.
