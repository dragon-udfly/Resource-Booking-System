# Internal Memo System User Guide

The **Internal Memo System** allows authorized officers to communicate securely within the system. It replaces physical notes for quick approvals, instructions, or inquiries.

## 1. Overview
The Internal Memo page is divided into three main sections:
1.  **Received Memos (Inbox)**: Messages sent to you.
2.  **Compose New Memo**: Form to send a new message.
3.  **Sent Memos (Outbox)**: Messages you have sent to others.

---

## 2. Sending a Memo
To send a message to another officer:
1.  Locate the **Compose New Memo** card (Green border).
2.  **To (Officer)**: Select the recipient from the dropdown list. It shows their Designation and Name.
3.  **Subject**: Enter a brief topic for the memo.
4.  **Description / Message**: Type your detailed message.
5.  Click **Send Memo**.
6.  Confirm the action in the popup dialog.

> **Note:** Once sent, the memo status is **Pending** until the receiver responds.

---

## 3. Receiving & Responding
When you receive a memo:
1.  It appears in your **Received Memos (Inbox)** list at the top.
2.  Click the **Open** button (or Eye icon) to open the message.
3.  Read the content in the popup window.

### Giving a Response
If the memo requires a decision, you will see two buttons at the bottom of the popup:
*   **YES (Green)**: Use this to **Agree**, **Approve**, or **Confirm** the request.
*   **NO (Red)**: Use this to **Disagree**, **Reject**, or **Deny** the request.

Once you click a response, the status is updated immediately for you and the sender.

---

## 4. Tracking Status (Sent Memos)
You can track the memos you sent in the **Sent Memos** section at the bottom.
*   **Pending**: The receiver has not yet responded.
*   **OK / Agreed**: The receiver clicked **YES**.
*   **No / Disagreed**: The receiver clicked **NO**.

> The system checks for updates every few seconds, so you don't need to refresh the page to see new responses.

---

## 5. Cleaning Up (Clear Buttons)
To keep your lists organized, you can remove old messages.

### Clear Read (Inbox)
*   Click the **Clear Read** button in the Received Memos header.
*   This will remove all memos that have been **Responded To** (Completed).
*   **It will NOT remove Pending memos.** You must respond to a memo before you can clear it.

### Clear Sent (Outbox)
*   Click the **Clear Sent** button in the Sent Memos header.
*   This will remove all memos where the receiver has **Already Responded**.
*   **It will NOT remove Pending memos.** You cannot clear a sent memo until the receiver gives an answer.
